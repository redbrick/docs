# Services Exposed to the Internet

## Aperture

- [Searxng](https://search.redbrick.dcu.ie) - Open source search engine designed around privacy.
- [Hedgedoc](https://md.redbrick.dcu.ie) - Documentation on hedgedoc is available [here](md).
- [Redbrick Wiki](https://wiki.redbrick.dcu.ie) - Full of historical information about redbrick, including a full timeline of our committee.
- Minecraft Servers - We run a vanilla minecraft server that is reset yearly for all of our members and also run individual servers for members upon request
- [Gatus](https://status.redbrick.dcu.ie) - Status page for all of our services and aperture.
- [Matrix](https://matrix.redbrick.dcu.ie) - Alternative to Discord, bridged with our discord server using a bot.
- [Mail](https://webmail.redbrick.dcu.ie/) - Every redbrick member gets their own redbrick email address.
- [Privatebin](https://paste.rb.dcu.ie) - Pretty much just pastebin.
- Webtree - We offer webhosting for all our members using the `public_html` folders in their home dirs. You can access the folder using our [login boxes](servers). Your site is then accessible at `your_username.redbrick.dcu.ie`.
## Sewer

- [OpenWebUI](https://ai.rb.dcu.ie) - Self hosted AI models with an API.
